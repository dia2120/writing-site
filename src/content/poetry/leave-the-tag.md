Well I can’t wear the new dress
Because my room isn’t clean
And I’m not clean either,
So I haven’t earned it yet

And I can’t start reading now,
Because I haven’t done the dishes
Or, actually, any of my assignments
And there’s dust collecting on top of my bookshelf,
Which I should really clean
Or should have cleaned yesterday,
I have people coming over tomorrow,
I really need to clean everything
So I haven’t earned rest

I haven’t earned the rest
And I haven’t earned lunch
Because if I wanted to eat,
I should have eaten earlier,
At 1:00, now it’s 4, and it’s not lunch time anymore
Now I have to wait for dinner time, which is after 6,
But before I eat dinner 
I need to dissect my entire being
And stalk someone’s LinkedIn
And realize I am falling incredibly behind
Very, very behind
And I haven’t earned the rest

And maybe I haven’t earned anything at all
So maybe I’ll actually just sit here
And do nothing
And just think about how much doing nothing is costing
Me, and while everyone else is moving and doing
I’ll sit and let my vision spiral, spiral, spiral and my hearing stop
Till I’m gone

But if I haven’t earned it today
Maybe I will tomorrow, and until then
I’ll leave the tag on the dress
and wait
