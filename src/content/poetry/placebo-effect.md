---
title: "Placebo Effect"
date: 2026-11-19
tags: []
---

Our shadows collided on the floor
and that's the closest I've ever felt to you.
My red ink on the tissue looked like blood and
I didn't really bother correcting you. If I wish
on a shooting star and then later realize it's a 
plane, does the wish not still count?
I'm convinced that if I learn every bone in the
human body, that puts me in the position of 
knowing you as well.
Once I told you we lose 1000 skin cells every minute
and you told me it was actually 40,000. 
Maybe by the time we've both shed enough of ourselves 
to become two new people, I'll be okay with being strangers.
