---
title: "Why I Love College"
date: 2026-11-11
tags: []
---

"how lovely is it?
that we don't have mortgages yet"
the fog that clouds our brain is
an assignment, or a shared hangover.

the air is warmly frigid
leaves wrap around us like curtains
how do they always narrowly miss us?
i wonder

your bright face opens words
that have nothing to do with me
i swallow them whole
within them rings some bell
that makes me smile anyway
maybe that is love

i think it is lovely
that we don't have mortgages yet;
we are two bowling balls gliding
down sidewalk, together, toward the
same pins.
