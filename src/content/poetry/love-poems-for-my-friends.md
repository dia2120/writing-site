1) 

Stars flicker in a flash
and hope to ricochet
off milky feathers, thoughtfully placed,
on the swan on the lake
Her beak is flush like petals
And pucker in something sprightly
But the sounds are not pretty bells,
They too, hold a universe within them

Her wings, set gentle, in motion
Ripples of endearment
They bounce and refract,
And replenish in waves
Of their compassion,
And warmly, one and the same
The swan on the lake
Embodies all of her name!

2)

In that frigid air
that bites at your skin,
The spark of the lighter,
Bright and warm
Melts into finger-tips

Under the hardened earthen rock,
Forged, even in pressure, unbroken
The hot, vibrant magma
Bellowing and firm
Will make itself seen

Filtered through the curtains,
Rising past the night
The soft, solar greeting
Constant and true
Is exactly what you need

3)

Until
Hot oil jumps off the iron
Sizzling laughs, buzzing
And the water in the vat
Boils, and bubbles with glee
the minutes move longer 
in wait for this

And while
Warm skin kneads dough
Knowing caress into the counter
Softens under palms
Like the sauce gets thicker
Thyme in the air 
lingers slower
