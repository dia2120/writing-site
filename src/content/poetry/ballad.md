date: 2026-05-31

There is a ghost haunting this room;
I’m very sure of it.
There is a cold breeze above the cushion,
Where you used to sit.

I sometimes hear a laugh,
And in the corner of my eye,
I see a familiar shadow
Slowly floating by.

In bed there is a second beating,
Pounding next to mine.
But I can’t say that I mind it,
Nearly half the time. 

She can be quite a nuisance,
With her mocking schemes
She often leaves your books and things
Right by me, just to see.

I once asked her to leave,
To give me a peace of heart,
But then I find her presence
a crutch from which I cannot part.

So in the evenings I leave the window open,
And set out two cups for tea,
This way things aren’t as empty,
As they used to be.
