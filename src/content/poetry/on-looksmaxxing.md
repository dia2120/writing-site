date: 2026-06-15

I saw the best minds of my generation destroyed
By reflections, sharp and taking 
Refracting faces, retreating 
Into a dawnless cage.

I saw the best minds of my generation absent,
From inner thought, potential and living
Unseen and thus, pointless
Turned towards the outer.

I saw the best minds of my generation lost,
In digital microcosms, preying and calculating
Hammers and shots, injecting
What will return back to nothing.

I saw the best minds of my generation question,
What is a mind?
In the world of walking mirrors, 
Looking upon each other, where there is no inside.
